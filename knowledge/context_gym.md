# Gym and training

## Energy
Training music sits at 0.9 energy and above. Below 0.8 the track stops pushing
and starts accompanying, which is the wrong job in a gym.

## Tempo
130 to 165 bpm. A running cadence is roughly 160 steps per minute, so tracks in
that range synchronise with the movement instead of fighting it.

## What to avoid
Slow acoustic material, anything under 0.5 energy, and quiet piano. Save those
for the walk home.
