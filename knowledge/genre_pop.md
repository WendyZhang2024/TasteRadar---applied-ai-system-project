# Pop

## Energy
Pop in this catalog runs from 0.76 to 0.93. The genre is built around a chorus
that arrives on time, so the energy curve is shaped on purpose rather than flat.

## Structure
Verse, chorus, verse, chorus, bridge. Hooks repeat every 20 to 30 seconds. That
structure is why pop is easy to remember after one listen and hard to ignore
while working.

## Watch out
A genre match alone is worth 2.0 points, so pop floods the top of the list for
anyone who names it, even when the mood and the energy are both wrong.
