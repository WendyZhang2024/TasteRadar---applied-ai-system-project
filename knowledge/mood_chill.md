# Chill

## Energy
Chill lives between 0.25 and 0.45 energy. It is a mood about pace, not about
volume: a chill track can be warm and full without being loud.

## Chill versus calm
Calm is quieter and stiller; chill still has a pulse. A calm track can sit at
0.12, while a chill track usually keeps a slow beat going underneath.

## Reach
Chill crosses genres: ambient, lo-fi and indie all have chill entries, which is
why a mood match often surfaces songs from outside the requested genre.
