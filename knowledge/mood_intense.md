# Intense

## Energy
Intense starts around 0.85 and stays there. In this catalog the intense tracks
sit at 0.91, 0.93 and 0.95, all above 130 bpm.

## Intense versus aggressive
Aggressive adds distortion and shout; intense is about pressure and drive. The
distinction matters because both land at the loud end of the scale.

## When it fits
Training, commuting through a crowd, any moment that needs to be pushed rather
than soothed.
