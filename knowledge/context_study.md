# Studying, coding and deep work

## Energy
Music for study and coding sits between 0.25 and 0.42 energy. Loud enough to
mask a noisy room, quiet enough that nothing jumps out. In this catalog the
three lo-fi tracks land at 0.35, 0.40 and 0.42, which is why they keep winning
this request.

## Texture
Repetitive loops and few or no lyrics. Lyrics compete with reading and with
writing code, which is the same language machinery. Steady tempo, no sudden
dynamic jumps.

## What to avoid
Anything above 0.6 energy pulls attention away from the task. Tracks with dense
vocals do the same even at low energy. Late at night the floor drops further:
0.28 to 0.35 works better than 0.45.
