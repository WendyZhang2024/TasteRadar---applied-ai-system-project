# Lo-fi

## Energy
The three lo-fi tracks in this catalog sit at 0.35, 0.40 and 0.42. Lo-fi is
built to sit under other activity, so it rarely goes above 0.5 and never reaches
the top of the energy scale.

## Texture
Dusty samples, soft drums, tape hiss, few vocals. Tempo stays between 72 and 80
bpm. The loop is the point: repetition is what makes it easy to ignore.

## Why it wins background requests
Because it asks for nothing. A listener who wants music while doing something
else is not choosing a song, they are choosing a texture.
